💻 ECU joins AMCRC as research partner in Australian AM network
Edith Cowan University (ECU) has joined the Additive Manufacturing Cooperative Research Centre (AMCRC) as its newest research partner, the organization has confirmed.
In doing so, ECU enters a network of 12 universities and the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO), reported by VoxelMatters on Oct 03, 2026.

🖨 PharmaTher enters North American deal with Craft Health
PharmaTher has become the exclusive North American distributor of the CraftMake pharmaceutical 3D printing platform under an agreement with Singapore-based Craft Health Pte.
The deal covers CraftMake printers, CraftControl software, CraftBlends formulations, consumables and service, reported by VoxelMatters on Oct 03, 2026.

🚀 ESA’s ISS metal printer produces further thrusters made in space
The European Space Agency (ESA) has reported that the fifth metal sample printed on the International Space Station (ISS) returned to Earth carrying the first thrusters created in space.
ESA astronaut Sophie Adenot retrieved the sample in July from ESA’s metal 3D printer, which operates in the station’s Columbus module, reported by VoxelMatters on Oct 02, 2026.

🏠 Fake plant company Nearly Natural expands into 3D printed décor
Nearly Natural, a popular US-based brand of artificial plants, is expanding its product offering with the recent acquisition of Modernized Pottery, a company that specializes in 3D printed planters and home décor.
Based out of Texas, Modernized Pottery has been making and selling a range of planters, vases, and more since 2023, reported by VoxelMatters on Oct 02, 2026.
